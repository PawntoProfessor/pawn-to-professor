WORLD FOOD TRAVEL — HTML5 TOUCHSCREEN GAME

How to host:
1. Upload the entire world-food-travel folder to your web host.
2. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
3. Open index.html in a modern browser.

Gameplay:
- 2–4 players choose Foodie tokens.
- 8 vocabulary cards shuffle once at the start and then stay fixed.
- Correct card: move +1 and the same player continues.
- Wrong card: move -1 and the turn ends.
- A player can never move behind START.
- First player to reach space 24 wins.

Sound:
- Game effects are generated locally by the browser.
- Vocabulary pronunciation uses the browser's speech synthesis voice when available.
- The opening tap unlocks browser audio.

No external JavaScript libraries or internet APIs are required for gameplay.
